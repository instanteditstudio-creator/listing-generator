Pricing strategy for LoveyTemplatesCo

Your current $9.99 price is actually strong for a new shop. Here is how to position it:

  • Keep $9.99 for premium designs — Your Talk Thirty To Me and detailed illustrated designs justify this price. Don't discount these.
  • Add a $5-7 tier — Simpler single-page designs. Easier entry point that builds reviews faster. More reviews = more trust = more $9.99 sales.
  • Create $14-18 bundles — Invitation + thank you + party sign bundle. Higher value, higher perceived quality. Buyers feel they are getting a deal.
  • Run a launch sale at 40% off — Your first 10 sales are crucial for Etsy algorithm. Temporarily price at $5.99 to get initial reviews and momentum.
  • Never go below $4 — Low prices signal low quality on Etsy. You want buyers who value quality.
